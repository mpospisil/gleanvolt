Sessions and history

Gleanvolt records every charge it controls, from the moment a mode starts charging the car until it stops. Sessions lists them.

The list

Section titled “The list”

Each row is one charge:

  • Started: when, in your time zone. Click it to open the charge.
  • Duration: how long it ran.
  • Strategy: the charging mode.
  • Delivered: the energy that went into the car, measured at the charger.
  • Solar share: how much of it came from your own panels.

Use Show to choose how far back the list goes.

One charge

Section titled “One charge”

At the top:

  • When, how long, the mode, and the end reason: why it stopped. For example “The car finished charging”, “The energy asked for was delivered”, “The car was unplugged”, “No sun was left to charge from today”, or “Charge control was switched to Off”.
  • The home battery level at the start and the end, and the peak charging power.
  • The weather at the start and the end, and the day’s daylight, if you have set up Weather.

Energy source split shows where the car’s energy came from: the sun, the grid, or the home battery. In Forecasted mode, energy the battery lent to the car is mentioned separately.

The chart

Section titled “The chart”

The chart shows the whole charge, in watts:

  • Solar, the forecast, the grid, the home battery and the EV charger (the car).
  • The surplus: the sun the house didn’t need.
  • The home battery level, on the right-hand scale.
  • In Forecasted mode, the SOC floor, the level the battery must stay above, and the battery loan.
  • A shaded band where the battery discharge hold was on.
  • A dashed line where something happened, such as the mode changing or the charge pausing. Each one is listed under the chart.

For Grid, buying is above the zero line and selling below. For Battery, charging is above and discharging below. Click a name in the legend to hide that line, and point at the chart to read the values.

If the car’s battery level was being read, its percentage is drawn too.

How long history is kept

Section titled “How long history is kept”

Charges and the energy history are kept on the computer Gleanvolt runs on, in its data folder. They are part of your backup.
