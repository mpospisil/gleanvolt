---
title: When the grid meter misses a phase
description: Why a grid meter that doesn't measure one phase stops the solar modes, how to tell, and what to enter.
---

**In short: leave the "Unmetered grid phase" setting empty**, unless your grid meter really doesn't
measure one of the three phases. On a correctly wired installation, setting it makes things worse.

## The problem

A three-phase inverter has a **grid meter** where your house connects to the grid: a smart meter, or
current clamps on the cables, measuring what flows in and out on each phase (L1, L2 and L3; SolaX calls
them R, S and T).

Gleanvolt works out the surplus from that meter. If the clamp on one phase is missing, loose or on the
wrong cable, the meter reads **0 W on that phase**, whatever really flows there. Then:

- **About a third of the sun you export is invisible.** Gleanvolt thinks the house uses it, finds little
  or no surplus, and the solar modes don't start the car, even on a sunny afternoon with a full battery.
- **About a third of the car's draw is invisible.** Gleanvolt thinks the sun covers the car while it
  quietly charges from the grid.

## Signs to look for

- A sunny afternoon, the home battery full, and yet the Overview shows a large **House** figure and
  almost no surplus. Solar + grid or Forecasted doesn't start.
- The **House** figure rises and falls with the sun.
- While the car charges, the House figure shows a dash, or the grid import is smaller than what the car
  alone takes.
- Your electricity bill shows more bought electricity than Gleanvolt or your inverter's app.

:::note
A big jump in the House figure for a while is usually a big appliance. An electric oven, for example,
takes several kilowatts.
:::

## How to check

Look at the grid meter's readings **per phase** while the sun shines: on the inverter's display, in its
app, or ask your installer.

- **Wired correctly:** all three phases show values that change.
- **One phase blind:** one phase stays at **0 W** all the time, while the inverter's own output on that
  phase clearly isn't zero.

If you can't check it, leave the setting empty and ask your installer.

## What to enter

On **Settings**, under the inverter, enter the blind phase in **Unmetered grid phase**: `L1`, `L2` or
`L3` (or SolaX's `R`, `S` or `T`). Press **Save**, then **Restart**.

Gleanvolt then estimates that phase from what it does know: the inverter's own output on it, the car's
share of the charge, and the house's use on the other two phases. It is an estimate, and a big
appliance on that phase puts it off for a while, but it is far better than a third of everything missing.

## The real fix

Have an electrician fit or fix the clamp. When the meter measures all three phases, **empty the
setting** and restart. Left set on a meter that sees every phase, it counts that phase twice.
