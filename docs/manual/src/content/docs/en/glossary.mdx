---
title: Glossary
description: The words used in Gleanvolt and in this manual, explained.
---

## A (ampere), current
How much electricity flows. The charger is set in amperes. On three phases, each ampere is about
690 W; on one phase, about 230 W.

## Battery discharge hold
Stops the home battery from powering the house and the car, while it can still charge from the sun.
See [Battery discharge hold](../using/battery-hold/).

## Battery loan
In [Forecasted](../using/modes/forecasted/) mode, power the home battery gives the car when the sun is
just short of the car's minimum, on a day the forecast says the battery will be refilled.

## Charger, wallbox
The box on the wall the car's cable plugs into. Gleanvolt sets how much current it gives the car.

## Charging mode
How Gleanvolt charges the car: Solar, Forecasted, Solar + grid, Fast (no battery) or Targeted. See
[Which mode should I choose?](../using/modes/choosing/)

## Departure, Ready by
When you need the car. Fast and Targeted can plan a charge to finish by then.

## Export, import
Selling electricity to the grid, and buying it from the grid.

## Forecast, p10, p90
How much sun Solcast expects. The expected value is as likely to be beaten as missed; **p10** is a
cloudier outcome (only 1 day in 10 is worse), **p90** a sunnier one.

## HDO, low tariff, high tariff
A two-price electricity contract: cheaper at certain hours (low tariff), usually at night. In the Czech
Republic it's switched by the distributor's HDO signal. See [Grid tariff](../setting-up/grid-tariff/).

## Home battery
The battery connected to your inverter, which stores sun for the evening. Not the car's battery.

## House
What your house uses, not counting the car. Gleanvolt works it out from the other figures.

## Inverter
The device that turns your panels' power into household electricity and manages the home battery.

## kW, kWh
kW (kilowatt) is power: how fast, at this moment. kWh (kilowatt-hour) is energy: how much in total. A
car charging at 11 kW for two hours takes 22 kWh.

## Minimum current, 6 A
The least current a car can charge on: about 4.1 kW on three phases, 1.4 kW on one. Below it, the car
pauses. See [How Gleanvolt decides](../how-it-works/basics/).

## Modbus TCP
The way Gleanvolt talks to the inverter and the charger over your home network. It must be turned on
in each device. See [Preparing your inverter and charger](../installation/preparing-devices/).

## Phase, L1, L2, L3
Most homes in Europe have three-phase electricity: three separate wires, called L1, L2 and L3 (SolaX:
R, S and T). A car charges on one phase or on all three.

## Recovery key
A code shown once when you set your Gleanvolt password, to reset the password if you forget it.

## Session
One charge, from start to stop. See [Sessions and history](../using/sessions/).

## SOC (state of charge)
How full a battery is, in percent. Used for both the home battery and the car's battery.

## SOC floor
The home battery level Forecasted mode won't let the car take it below, so it can still be full by the
evening.

## Solcast
The service that provides the solar forecast. Free for home use. See
[Solar forecast](../setting-up/solar-forecast/).

## Standing down
Switching the charger to Stop during a long wait, such as overnight, instead of pausing it.

## Surplus
The sun your house doesn't use at this moment: what the panels make minus what the house uses. What the
solar modes give the car.

## Unmetered grid phase
A phase your grid meter doesn't measure. Leave the setting empty unless that is really the case. See
[When the grid meter misses a phase](../how-it-works/unmetered-phase/).

## VIN
The car's vehicle identification number, 17 letters and digits, shown in the car's papers and app.
