---
title: Welcome
description: What Gleanvolt does, what it doesn't, and what you need to use it.
---

**Gleanvolt charges your electric car from your own solar panels.** It runs on a small computer in
your home, such as a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or a Windows PC. From there it talks to your inverter and your
car charger over your home network, and decides, moment by moment, how fast the car should charge.
You use it from a web page, on your phone or your computer.

## What it does

- **Charges the car from sunshine** that your house isn't using, instead of sending it to the grid.
- **Gives you a choice of charging modes**: from the sun only, from the sun with a little help from the
  grid, as fast as possible, or a set amount by a set time. You pick one with one tap.
- **Keeps the home battery out of it** when you want it to, so the car doesn't empty the battery
  you'll need tonight.
- **Shows you what is happening**: how much the sun makes, what the house uses, what goes into the car,
  and a history of every charge.
- **Works without the internet.** Everything happens on your home network. The internet is only used
  for the optional solar forecast, the weather, and your car's battery level.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't replace your inverter's or your charger's own app. It only sets how fast the car charges
  and whether the home battery may discharge.
- It doesn't run in the cloud, and it has no account to sign up for.
- It doesn't work with every inverter and charger. See the list below.

## What you need

- **A supported inverter**: a SolaX hybrid inverter, or a Huawei SUN2000. Some inverters sold under
  other names (Tigo, Qcells, Dražice) are SolaX inside and work too.
- **A supported car charger**: a SolaX HAC or EVC, a Huawei SCharger, or a go-e.
- **A computer that stays on**, in the same home network as the inverter and the charger: a Raspberry
  Pi (64-bit), a NAS, any 64-bit Linux machine, or a Windows PC.
- **A home network** that the inverter and the charger are connected to, by cable or Wi-Fi.
- **Any electric car** with a Type 2 charging socket. Reading the car's battery level is optional;
  it works for Volkswagen and Škoda cars, and for others through Home Assistant.

## Use at your own risk

Gleanvolt changes how your charger and your inverter behave. It has been tested carefully, but it is
free software that comes with no warranty. Keep an eye on the first few charges, and if anything looks
wrong, set the charging mode to **Off**.

:::note[For advanced users]
Home Assistant, MQTT, the HTTP API and the history database are documented in the
[Gleanvolt repository on GitHub](https://github.com/mpospisil/gleanvolt).
:::
