Gleanvolt talks to your inverter and your charger over your home network. Most devices have this
switched **off** when they leave the factory, so check it before you install.

What to check depends on the brand:

| Device | What to turn on | Where |
|---|---|---|
| SolaX inverter | Modbus TCP | The inverter's settings |
| SolaX charger (HAC, EVC) | Modbus TCP | The charger's settings |
| Huawei SUN2000 inverter | Modbus TCP, **Enable (Unrestricted)** | FusionSolar app, as the installer |
| Huawei SCharger | Modbus TCP | FusionSolar app |
| go-e charger | **Local HTTP API v2** | go-e app |

Both devices also need to be **connected to your home network**, by cable or Wi-Fi, and switched on.

## SolaX inverter and charger

Turn on **Modbus TCP** in the inverter's settings, and again in the charger's. Where the setting is
depends on the model and its firmware. If you can't find it, ask your installer: it may be behind an
installer password.

:::note
If the charger is linked to the inverter for SolaX's own "Green" or "ECO" charging, unlink it.
Gleanvolt needs to talk to the charger directly.
:::

## Huawei SUN2000 inverter

You need the **installer account** for the FusionSolar app. Ask your installer if you don't have it.

<Steps>

1. In the FusionSolar app, open **Device commissioning** for the inverter and sign in as the installer.

2. If the inverter is connected through an **SDongle**: go to **Settings → Communication configuration →
   Dongle parameter settings → Modbus TCP** and choose **Enable (Unrestricted)**.

   If it is connected through an **EMMA**: open the EMMA's commissioning page instead, and turn on Modbus TCP
   under **Commission device → Communication settings → Modbus TCP**.

</Steps>

You also need a **Huawei smart meter** at the grid connection (DTSU666-H or DDSU666-H), or an EMMA, which
has one built in. Without it, Gleanvolt can't see how much power goes to or comes from the grid.

If you use an EMMA and want Gleanvolt to hold the home battery, switch off the EMMA's **AI energy
optimisation** and set it to **maximum self-consumption**. Only one system may control the battery.

## Huawei SCharger

In the FusionSolar app, open the charger's commissioning page, and turn on Modbus TCP under
**Settings → Communication configuration**.

If the charger is managed by an EMMA, leave it to the EMMA for now: Gleanvolt can read it but should not
control it.

## go-e charger

<Steps>

1. In the go-e app, open your charger, then **Internet → Advanced settings**.

2. Switch on **Local HTTP API v2**.

3. If you use the app's own solar charging ("Eco" mode), switch it off. Gleanvolt does that job now.

</Steps>

This needs a go-e with hardware version 3 or later: a Gemini, Gemini flex, HOMEfix or HOME+ V3 or V4.

## Only one controller at a time

A device can only be controlled by one program. If you already use one of these, switch it off for the
devices Gleanvolt will control:

- **Home Assistant**'s SolaX or Huawei Solar integration. Some devices accept only one connection, and
  Gleanvolt keeps its connection open all the time.
- **evcc**, or any other charging controller.
- The charger app's own solar or scheduled charging.
