---
title: Privacy
description: What Gleanvolt keeps, where, and what leaves your home network, and when.
---

Gleanvolt runs on your own computer, in your own home. **There is no Gleanvolt account, no Gleanvolt
server, and nothing is sent to the author.** No usage statistics, no crash reports, no update checks.

Still, what Gleanvolt records is personal: a minute-by-minute picture of your electricity shows when
someone is home, when the car comes and goes, and when the household is awake. So here is exactly where
it goes.

## What is kept on your computer

Everything is in Gleanvolt's `data` and `logs` folders, on the computer it runs on.

| What | Contains | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| Charging sessions | Each charge: when, how much, from which source, the home battery level, and the car's battery level if it is read | 365 days |
| Energy history | Every quarter hour: solar, grid, home battery, car | Always, unless you delete it |
| Logs | What Gleanvolt decided and why, and errors | 14 days |
| Settings | Your settings, including your home's location and your devices' addresses | Until you delete them |
| Passwords and keys | Your car account's password, the Solcast and weather keys, and others | Until you remove them |

Gleanvolt never asks for your name, e-mail, address, phone number or payment details.

**Passwords and keys** are stored so only Gleanvolt can read them (on Windows, only your Windows
account), but they aren't encrypted in a way a copy of the folder couldn't use. Anyone with a copy of the
`data` folder could sign in to your car's account. Keep backups private.

**Your Gleanvolt password** is stored only as a scrambled hash, which can't be turned back into the
password.

**To delete everything**, stop Gleanvolt and delete its `data` folder.

## What stays in your home network

- **The inverter and the charger**: Gleanvolt talks to them directly, over your network.
- **The Gleanvolt page** (port 8090): shows nothing until a password is set, and asks for it after that.
  The connection isn't encrypted (plain HTTP), so use it only on a home network you trust.
- **Home Assistant**, if you connect it: the live figures go to the MQTT broker you name.

## What leaves your home, and when

Only these, and **only if you set them up**:

| Service | What is sent | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Solcast** (solar forecast) | Your Solcast key and the site id you created at Solcast. Not your location | A few times a day |
| **OpenWeatherMap** (weather) | Your key, and **your home's location** | Twice per charge |
| **Volkswagen, Škoda or the VW Group Data Act portal** (car battery level) | Your account's sign-in, and your car's VIN, to the car maker | While reading the car |

Requests to your car's maker go to them, under their privacy policy. Gleanvolt signs in as you, with the
details you gave it, and keeps only the car's battery level, range, plug and charge state, and when the
car reported them. Your VIN isn't stored in the history.

**Your home's location** leaves your network only if you set up the weather, which needs it.

Nothing else is ever contacted.
